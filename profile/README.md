# The Level Everything Company

**Build. Explore. Create. Level Everything.**

A creative technology workshop for independent software, open-source tools, experimentation, digital media, and odd ideas worth testing.

Founded by **Alex**, the creator behind [Wommper1](https://github.com/Wommper1) and earlier creative identities **Alex Shade Productions** and **Alex Shade Photography**.

> I built too many worlds to live inside just one.

## What happens here

- **Software that people can use:** independent projects, useful utilities, and experiments released openly when possible.
- **Curiosity in the real world:** local history, scanners, sensors, hardware, and overlooked details.
- **Creative technology:** film and audio tools, artificial intelligence, virtual worlds, and storytelling.
- **Field notes:** the useful parts of the process, including what breaks and what we learn.
